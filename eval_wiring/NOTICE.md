# Data attribution

Screenshots and case images here come from **FETAL_PLANES_DB** (Zenodo 3904280, doi:10.5281/zenodo.3904280, CC BY 4.0;
Burgos-Artizzu et al., Sci Rep 10:10200, 2020) and **HC18** (Zenodo 1327317, CC BY 4.0; van den Heuvel et al., PLoS ONE
13(8):e0200412, 2018). Case file paths in `cases*.json` point to local copies of those datasets.
